# Cookie policy

**Last updated:** [PUBLICATION DATE]

## 1. What they are

Cookies and similar technologies (such as browser local storage) store information on your device. This policy lists those used by Moodly's public website (moodlycrm.com) and the platform ([APP URL]).

## 2. Summary

**Moodly only uses technical cookies and storage, or preferences you choose.** No analytics, advertising or third-party cookies. That is why we do not show a consent banner: these cookies are exempt under Article 22.2 of the LSSI-CE because they are strictly necessary for the service you request or store a preference you chose. If we ever add cookies that need consent (e.g. analytics), we will update this policy and ask first.

## 3. Public website (moodlycrm.com)

| Name | Type | Set by | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Cookie | Moodly (first party) | Remember the language you choose with the language switcher. Only set if you switch language. | 1 year |
| `moodly-theme` | Local storage | Moodly | Remember light or dark theme. | Until you clear it |
| `moodly-plataforma-lang` | Local storage | Moodly | Language chosen inside the demo prototype (`/plataforma`). | Until you clear it |
| `moodly.sede` | Local storage | Moodly | Sample location chosen inside the demo prototype. | Until you clear it |

The contact and demo forms store nothing: they open your email program.

## 4. Platform (web app)

| Name | Type | Set by | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sb-[ref]-auth-token` (may be split into `.0`, `.1`…) | Cookie, `HttpOnly`, `Secure` | Moodly (first party, generated by the Supabase Auth library) | Keep you securely signed in. | [Per `@supabase/ssr` defaults; verify] |
| `sb-[ref]-auth-token-code-verifier` | Cookie | Moodly | Complete some sign-in flows securely (PKCE). Exists only during the flow. | Minutes [verify] |
| `org` | Cookie, `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax`, `Secure` | Moodly | Remember the active organisation if you belong to several. | 1 year |
| `theme` | Cookie, same flags | Moodly | Theme chosen in Preferences. | 1 year |
| `locale` | Cookie, same flags | Moodly | Language chosen in Preferences. | 1 year |
| `sidebar_collapsed` | Cookie, same flags | Moodly | Remember whether you collapsed the sidebar. | 1 year |
| `moodly-shell-v1` | Service worker cache | Moodly | Make the app installable and show an offline page. Stores only icons, the manifest and the offline page; never pages or data. | Until the next app version |

Error monitoring (Sentry) is configured not to use cookies or send yours.

## 5. Managing them

You can delete or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Blocking the platform's cookies prevents signing in; blocking preference cookies makes the site and app use defaults.
