A **template** for each business using Moodly to inform its clients (the business is the controller; Moodly its processor). Basis: Art. 50(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (applicable from 2 August 2026 [verify]) and GDPR Arts. 13 and 14.

# Transparency note: you are talking to an AI assistant

## A. In-conversation notice (said by the agent)

At the start of each new conversation, and whenever asked:

> Hi, I'm the virtual assistant of **[BUSINESS NAME]**. I'm an artificial intelligence system, not a person. I can help with [appointments, questions about services, opening hours…]. If you'd rather talk to someone on the team, just tell me and I'll pass you over. More information: [LINK TO NOTE].

The agent never denies being an AI; asking for a person always hands the conversation to a person; with voice (later), the notice is given at the start of the call and synthetic audio is marked.

## B. Short note (linked from the notice)

**[BUSINESS NAME]'s assistant**

- **What it is:** an AI assistant that answers messages and handles appointments and enquiries on behalf of [BUSINESS NAME], running on the Moodly platform.
- **What it does:** answers with the information the business has given it. If it doesn't know something, it doesn't make it up: it asks the team and gets back to you, or passes you to a person.
- **What it doesn't do:** it gives no medical, legal or tax advice and makes no important decisions about you without a person from the business. Payments, sending documents and sensitive changes need approval from someone on the team.
- **Talk to a person:** write "I want to talk to a person" at any time.
- **Your data:** [BUSINESS NAME] processes it as controller to handle your enquiry and appointments; Moodly processes it on its behalf as processor. To reply to you, the conversation text is sent to AI model providers, which **may process it outside the European Union** under GDPR safeguards. More information and your rights: [LINK TO THE BUSINESS'S PRIVACY POLICY].

## C. Paragraph for the business's privacy policy

> **AI assistant.** To answer the messages you send us via [WhatsApp / web chat / email], we use an AI assistant from the Moodly platform ([RAZÓN SOCIAL], [DOMICILIO SOCIAL]), which processes your data on our behalf as a processor. We process your name, phone or email, the conversation content and your appointment details to handle your enquiry and bookings (legal basis: our pre-contractual or contractual relationship with you, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) [and, if the assistant keeps preferences to serve you better in future, our legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f)]. The conversation text is sent to AI model providers, which may process it outside the European Economic Area under standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. We keep conversations for [period set by the business]. You can ask to talk to a person at any time and exercise your rights by writing to [BUSINESS CONTACT].
